Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Aventro UK Co. ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle information collected via our website (aventro-uk-co.github.io).

Data Collection

We collect data you voluntarily provide via our Contact Form (Name, Email, Subject, Message). This data is processed securely by Formspree (our form backend) and sent directly to our email inboxes for response purposes. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics that identify individual users.

Data Usage

Your information is used solely to respond to your inquiry (partnership, investment, research, or employment). We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage & Security

Data is transmitted via encrypted channels (SSL/TLS). While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Your Rights (UK GDPR)

As a UK-registered entity, you have the right to access, rectify, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@aventro.co.uk.
